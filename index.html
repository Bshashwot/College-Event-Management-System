College Event Management System

A web-based platform that helps students, administrators and colleges manage events efficiently. Students can register for approved events, administrators manage event activities and colleges approve events before publication.

About Our System

The College Event Management System is developed to simplify the organization of seminars, workshops, sports competitions, hackathons and cultural events. Students can easily view approved events, register online and receive notifications. Administrators manage events while colleges approve or reject event requests before they become available.
